## Locked architecture and decisions
1. [Admin flat-method contract](../../architecture/shipping/SHIPPING-ADMIN-FLAT-METHOD-CONTRACT.md): one selectable canonical method (e.g. `tipax:standard`) is one independent Admin row; provider shared metadata only. Add Service; View/Edit/Delete row actions; exactly **four non-nested tabs** General / Rates & Coverage / Transport Schedule / Packaging & Freight. Canonical method identity comes from authoritative **server binding** (serviceCode, optionCode), never from browser string-splitting.
2. Addendum in same contract: optional geography, dimensions/weight, days and other supported constraints are **unrestricted BY ADMIN when absent**, not automatically active, financially ready or carrier-guaranteed. Explicit Restricted-empty means deny all for that configured dimension, never unrestricted. Rates and policies never silently inherit from provider/service-level or sibling method. Enabled and quote-ready distinct. Fixed IRR proved; **FREE_TO_CUSTOMER, RULE_BASED_RATE, COLLECT_ON_DELIVERY, PROVIDER_QUOTE** must be modeled/validated according to real authority, not invented by UI or absent tariff; no unsupported free/zero quotes.
3. [Independent delivery commitments](../../architecture/shipping/SHIPPING-GROUP-INDEPENDENT-DELIVERY-COMMITMENT.md): each real shipment/seller group keeps stable `ShippingGroupId`, its own goods, selected method, fee, earliest eligible date and window; two sellers using the same method **remain two groups**, and editing one must not reset the other. Same seller+method with incompatible trusted origins still **BLOCKED** by absent origin authority / uniqueness model; never fabricate origins or bypass DB index.
4. [Fee allocation policy](../../architecture/checkout/SHIPPING-GROUP-FEE-ALLOCATION-POLICY-DECISION.md): independent payment shipping-group charges can persist, but allocation stays `PendingAllocation` pending commercial sign-off on carrier payable, seller/marketplace shares, tax, rounding, refund/reversal and policy version. No actual payout and no default guessed share.
5. Packaging charge **not approved for customer payable**: TB-177 demonstrates Shipping-owned packaging policy is consumed only by operational quote / default-OFF observation. Order accepted charge and Payment allocation do not include it; missing tax, customer-vs-operator payer rule and authoritative financial consumer. Do not add packaging to customer amount pending explicit business decision.
