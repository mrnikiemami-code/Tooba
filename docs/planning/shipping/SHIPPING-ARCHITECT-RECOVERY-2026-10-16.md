## Locked, non-negotiable architecture
1. [Admin flat methods](../../architecture/shipping/SHIPPING-ADMIN-FLAT-METHOD-CONTRACT.md): **ONE customer-selectable shipping method = ONE independently managed Admin service**, e.g. `post:express` and `post:standard` are different rows, never child editable types under a generic Post service. Provider/carrier metadata shared only; never inherit rates/coverage/schedule/packaging/finance policies from provider or sibling. Exactly View/Edit/Delete per row; Add Service action; inside a service four **non-nested** tabs: General; Rates & Coverage; Transport Schedule; Packaging & Freight. Retain canonical codes/bindings and historical order snapshots.
2. Same Admin contract LOCKED ADDENDUM 2026-10-16: optional restrictions absent = **UNRESTRICTED BY ADMIN** on all supported geography, weight, dimensions, days etc; restrictions are opt-in and affect ONLY the selected method. But **monetary status must be explicit** per method: free-to-customer, fixed IRR, rate rules, collect on delivery, or authorized provider quote. Missing price rule is NOT zero/free; enabled is distinct from financially quote-ready. Never invent carrier slots/payouts or override real physical eligibility.
3. [Independent shipping group commitments](../../architecture/shipping/SHIPPING-GROUP-INDEPENDENT-DELIVERY-COMMITMENT.md): **every seller's actual shipment is an independent obligation**, identified by stable `ShippingGroupId`. Equal method DOES NOT merge sellers. Seller A `tipax:standard` earliest +2 days and Seller B same `tipax:standard` earliest +3 days => **TWO cards**, each with their own items, seller, price, date and time-window picker, selectable only from that group's authoritative server projection. Even same date or same method does not merge distinct sellers; same seller + method with incompatible trusted origins/promises also must be split once domain authority exists. Never display one shared bottom-of-page schedule or intersect dates/windows across groups.
4. [Checkout grouping contract](../../architecture/checkout/SHIPPING-METHOD-GROUPING-UI-CONTRACT.md), above delivery-schedule addendum supersedes its former global-date logic. [Shipping group fee financial policy](../../architecture/checkout/SHIPPING-GROUP-FEE-ALLOCATION-POLICY-DECISION.md) remains undecided: PendingAllocation, no guessed seller/platform/carrier share or payout.
