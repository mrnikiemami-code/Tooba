# Owner policy — Offer inventory and fulfillment authority (2026-11-26)
Status: owner-confirmed business policy (conversation 2026-11-26); implementation pending. Isolated OpenCode branch; preserve main/Cursor governance.
## Confirmed
- Marketplace seller-fulfilled: seller may manage own eligible warehouse stock and own shipping policy within existing authorized scope. No access to another seller's warehouse.
- Marketplace platform-fulfilled: marketplace controls operational warehouse, physical receipts/counts/adjustments and shipping method/rate/time policy. Seller has no operational warehouse write or shipping-policy authority. Seller may see only stock belonging to their own offer and set how much of their own received stock to offer for sale (100 received, 20 offered); this is not a physical stock adjustment, warehouse choice or transfer. No grant from null owner. An explicit service/eligibility arrangement for platform fulfillment is not an operational warehouse-write grant.
- SingleStore: trusted active Store tenant owns its locations/shipping; no Marketplace/seller choice. Existing Platform fulfillment semantic maps to Store in SingleStore per StorefrontCapturedOwnerPolicy. No automatic fallback on unknown edition/tenant/owner/responsibility. Preserve existing server-side assignment authorization; seller cannot unilaterally impose platform fulfillment or edit platform shipping policy.
## Architecture decision for implementation
Inventory remains sole authority for physical, held and sellable quantity; docs/architecture/09-inventory-availability-reservation.md, 43-seller-offer-listing-foundation.md and 45-inventory-foundation.md prohibit stock columns on Offer. Offer owns the explicit fulfillment assignment, seller identity and presentation/orchestration; Shipping owns policy; Pricing owns price. Store sellable allocation/limit within Inventory and enforce it atomically with reservations, not via a duplicate Offer stock counter. Reuse existing OfferId/StockItemId identity, owner-local transaction/outbox and trusted tenant/edition. Do not introduce separate warehouse/shipping/inventory subsystems.
## Owner-confirmed quantity and technical implementation semantics
For platform-fulfilled offer seller declares remaining total allocated quantity, including held units. At 100 received, allocation 20, held 0 -> new-sale available 20. At held 5 -> new-sale available 15. Allocation can be increased within seller's own eligible physical stock or reduced no lower than protected held amount; no release of existing commitments. On normal sale consumption reduce physical, held and remaining allocation by consumed quantity exactly once; release restores availability within remaining allocation. Zero allocation stops new sales. Receipt/return must not silently increase a seller's explicit allocation. Physical losses must not invent physical stock, and existing paid-order supply/replacement/return policy remains intact; distinguish new-sale availability from existing committed-order recovery, prove no new-sale overcommit. Choose minimal owner-local representation and locking that enforces cap across all eligible positions for the same Offer; no per-location multiplication of an offer-wide limit. Cross-offer shared stock pools and automatic replenishment of the offered quantity are excluded. Fulfillment conversion with active commitments remains fail-closed unless separately authorized; never move stock or rewrite captured order responsibility automatically. Existing stock rows require explicit owner classification using reliable authority; unknown rows quarantined from seller mutation, not null=shared, no fake backfill. Test data can be recreated with explicit owner identities under standing DEV authorization.
