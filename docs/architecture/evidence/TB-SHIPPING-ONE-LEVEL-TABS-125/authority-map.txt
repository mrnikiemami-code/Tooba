2. FULFILLMENT — execution granularity (VERIFIED EXISTING, decisive)
================================================================================
FulfillmentDbContext.cs mapping:

  fulfillments  PK FulfillmentId
    HasIndex(SellerOrderId).IsUnique()        <-- EXACTLY ONE unit per SellerOrder TODAY
    HasIndex(SellerPartyId); HasIndex(CheckoutId)
    ShippingMethodCode nvarchar(64), ShippingMethodLabel nvarchar(128)
    + recipient/address snapshot

  shipments  PK ShipmentId
    HasIndex(FulfillmentId)                   <-- NOT unique => MANY shipments per unit
    CarrierDisplayName(128), ShippingMethodCode(64), ShippingMethodLabel(128),
    TrackingReference(128) unique filter "tracking_reference IS NOT NULL",
    PreviousTrackingReference(128), ProviderMetadataJson/Version
    => multiple shipments (and therefore multiple distinct methods) per SellerOrder
       are LEGAL TODAY inside ONE unit.

  shipment_items  PK ShipmentItemId; HasIndex(ShipmentId)
  consolidated_packages PK ConsolidatedPackageId; HasIndex(CheckoutId);
    PackageNumber unique; ShippingMethodCode(64)/Label(128); requires >=2 distinct
    sellers (domain). Admin post-purchase consolidation, NOT a customer grouping.
  consolidated_package_members HasIndex(ShipmentId).IsUnique() (filtered) =>
    a shipment may belong to at most one active package.

  shipping_methods PK ShippingMethodId; MethodCode unique; ProviderKind;
    LegacyServiceCode/LegacyOptionCode + unique (LegacyServiceCode, LegacyOptionCode)
    => canonical method identity ALREADY exists and is the right anchor for a group.

Domain (authority, not just schema):
  FulfillmentUnit.cs:26-59,85-120  unit carries ONE ShippingMethodCode (from checkout)
  FulfillmentUnit.CreateShipment(... shippingMethodCode, shippingMethodLabel ...):331-360
    -> per-shipment method is already supported (operator/seller supplied today)
  FulfillmentUnit.ProcessSelections/PackSelections/UnpackSelections/ApplyShipmentDispatched
    -> item membership and quantities are ALREADY tracked per order line inside the unit
  OrderFulfillmentBridge.cs:67-84 passes checkout.ShippingMethodCode/Label once, plus
    lines as (LineId, Quantity, ReservationId)

DECISIVE ANSWER: "multiple units per SellerOrder" is NOT legal today (unique index);
"multiple shipments, each with its own method, inside one unit" IS legal today.
=> the smallest owner-correct execution mapping is one Shipment per Order-owned
   shipping group inside the existing one-unit-per-seller boundary. No new
   Fulfillment aggregate is required.
