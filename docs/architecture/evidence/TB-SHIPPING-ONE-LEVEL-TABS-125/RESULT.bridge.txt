ALLOCATION AND RECONCILIATION RULE (MISSION B)
- Separation: Order owns the accepted quote snapshot; Settlement owns allocation and the ledger; Fulfillment owns execution and actual carrier cost; Payment/Wallet own money movement; Tax owns the shipping-tax rule; Host composes only.
- Invariant (fail-closed, never plugged by residual inference):
    CustomerChargeAmount - DiscountAmount == CarrierPayableAmount + SellerShareAmount + MarketplaceShareAmount + ShippingTaxAmount
  All amounts are the amount CREDITED to that party; SellerShareAmount and MarketplaceShareAmount are SIGNED (negative seller = seller bears shipping; negative marketplace = subsidy, explicitly allowed); CarrierPayableAmount >= 0; the discount funder is STORED, not inferred.
- Storage per group: customer charge, discount + typed funder, carrier payable, signed seller share, signed marketplace share, shipping tax (+ rule reference), currency, QuotePolicyVersion, AllocationPolicyVersion, State, PostedAt, with one live allocation per group and unique idempotency keys.
- Order rollup: aggregate customer shipping = sum of group net charges; never fee x group count and never double-added with the legacy scalar.
- Quote vs actual: Fulfillment-reported real carrier cost becomes a shipping_fee_adjustments row (Kind=CarrierCostActualVsQuoted) plus a linked entry; the original allocation is never overwritten.
- Reversal/refund: per-group Reversed + compensating entry, mirroring the existing per-SellerOrder cancel/restore discipline; a replayed posting is rejected by the idempotency key.
- NO percentage, tariff or commercial split is invented. Ledger shape (dedicated Settlement-owned shipping rows vs extending SettlementEntry) is decision (e); the recommendation is dedicated rows because SettlementEntry's gross x rate semantics must stay merchandise-only.
