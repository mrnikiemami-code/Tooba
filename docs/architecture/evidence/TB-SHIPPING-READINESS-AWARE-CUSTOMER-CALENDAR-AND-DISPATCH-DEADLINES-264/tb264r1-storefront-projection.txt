Reading of that live answer, stated without invention:

* The seller group is quoted from the **seller-owned** selected capture
  (`01a186f7-…`, source revision 10, selection version 11), NOT from the Store-owned
  `post/standard` method that this task saved and published. A buyer cart never borrows another
  owner's method — the owner boundary is intact.
* That seller capture has no carrier acceptance week, so the storefront produces
  `availableDates: []` with `storefront.captured_group.carrier_schedule_missing`. It refuses to
  invent a customer date rather than falling back to the old `leadDays` estimate.
* Consequence for issue D: the canonical catalog has no **Store-owned** in-stock offer, so a live
  positive customer-date row for the Store-owned method could not be minted without writing a seller's
  real shipping configuration or a new Store offer. Neither is authorised, so it was not done. The
  positive calendar/deadline chain is proven by the 12 authority facts plus the 13 capture/quote facts
  (Shipping 32/32 green) and by the format-4 payload above, which is exactly the input that authority
  consumes (see `tb264r1-connected-proof.txt`).
