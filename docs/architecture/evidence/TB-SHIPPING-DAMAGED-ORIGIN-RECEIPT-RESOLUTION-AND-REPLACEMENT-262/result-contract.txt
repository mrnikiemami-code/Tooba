LANDED 1 — Fulfillment owns the physical decision
New aggregate DamagedGoodsResolution (fulfillment.damaged_goods_resolutions): source shipment_id, shipment_return_receipt_id, fulfillment_id, order_line_id, actual damaged quantity, outcome (Replacement|Referral), status (Recorded|Reserved|Fulfilled|Cancelled), decided_by_user_id, occurrence_key, attempt, note, timestamps, and the linked replacement_reservation_id / replacement_shipment_id. Unique ux_damaged_goods_resolutions_fulfillment_occurrence (fulfillment_id, occurrence_key) is the durable identity, so a retried reviewed intent can only answer the recorded decision.
Quantity conservation is asserted: ReceiptDamagedQuantity(sourceParcel,line)=Σ receipt items condition Damaged; DamagedCommittedQuantity=Σ decisions status!=Cancelled; OutstandingDamagedQuantity=max(0, ReceiptDamaged−DamagedCommitted); ResendableQuantity(line)=ReturnedUsableQuantity−ResendCommittedQuantity (TB-258 unchanged). Damaged units are never usable/resend capacity, a decision is bounded by its OWN source parcel's outstanding damaged quantity AND by the paid-order obligation still undelivered (no sibling lending/borrowing), and one damaged unit funds exactly one decision (second decision = fulfillment.damage.exceeds_outstanding). Shipment gained DamagedResolutionId, ReplacementReservationId, IsReplacement, distinct from IsResend; a replacement parcel links BOTH the immediate damaged source (ResendOfShipmentId) and the exact resolution/reservation. The cancelled-attempt dispatch key uses attempt{N}:{resolutionId:N}, while the Inventory reservation key stays stable across attempts.
New typed localized codes fulfillment.damage.{resolution_ids_required, resolution_qty_positive, resolution_outcome_invalid, resolution_actor_required, resolution_occurrence_required, resolution_note_invalid, resolution_intent_conflict, source_not_returned, receipt_missing, line_foreign, no_damaged_quantity, exceeds_outstanding, obligation_already_settled, replacement_unavailable, original_reservation_missing, resolution_cancelled, resolution_not_replacement, resolution_not_open, resolution_not_found, reservation_required, reservation_conflict, replacement_conflict, replacement_missing, replacement_source_required, replacement_occurrence_required, concurrency_conflict} exist in both resx files with Persian copy, so no raw code reaches the operator.
