ROOT CAUSE (fixed): a Shipping rate policy was identified only by serviceCode, while the storefront
exposes methods as {serviceCode}:{optionCode}. A service with two options at two legacy prices therefore
had no reachable parity path: 068 measured tipax:standard 180000 matching a single service-level tipax
policy, and tipax:express 260000 structurally unreachable -> AmountMismatch. This was a policy-identity
defect, not a data gap.

CANONICAL IDENTITY + PRECEDENCE: policy identity is now (serviceCode, optionCode?, destination scope);
a null optionCode is the service-level policy and keeps the exact meaning it had for every pre-existing
row. Resolution order is deterministic and total: (1) option tier first - if any active, covering,
option-matching policy is option-specific, resolution happens inside that tier alone; (2) deepest
destination scope inside the winning tier; (3) two equally specific rows in the winning tier remain
ScopeConflict (never "first row wins"). A policy for one option can never price another option, and a
request with no optionCode never matches an option-specific policy, so every pre-069 caller is
byte-for-byte unchanged. methodCode is never equated with serviceCode anywhere: the projection carries
OptionCode as its own field (StorefrontShippingMethodView.OptionCode), the shadow candidate carries it
separately, and the observer passes ServiceCode + OptionCode to ShippingQuoteRequest.ShippingQuoteOptionCode.
COVERAGE SEMANTICS PRESERVED (explicit): coverage stays a service+destination grant; one grant authorizes
the whole service at that destination including all of its options. Options are a price dimension, not an
authorization dimension; per-option grants would silently shrink already-authorized coverage. Coverage is
NOT widened by option identity and the engine stays fail-closed (NotCovered without a grant).
