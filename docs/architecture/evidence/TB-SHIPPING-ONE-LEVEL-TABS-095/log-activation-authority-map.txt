The binding is already resolved server-side (086 binding route) and is never taken from the client.

=== CONCLUSION ===
A narrow activation of "post:express" cannot be made genuinely customer-visible without also flipping
fulfillment.shipping_service_options(code=express, service=post).is_active, and the only existing writer
for that column is the destructive, identity-rewriting legacy UpdateAsync, which this task forbids.
A method-row-only toggle is therefore a MISLEADING apparent activation and is not implemented.
