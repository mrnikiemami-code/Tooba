== 3. SELECTOR-VS-CONCRETE PROOF ==

Single authority, no duplicated policy. Both the resolver and StorefrontPaymentOrchestrator derive the concrete
provider from the SAME member `IPaymentGatewayCatalogPort.DefaultProvider`, guarded by the SAME
`IsOnlineGatewayOffered()` call:
  - Orchestrator (unchanged): default at :124; gateway-alias branch at :149-151 re-assigns `_catalog.DefaultProvider`
    after the `IsOnlineGatewayOffered()` guard; manual branch at :141-144 gates on `ManualCardToCardEnabled`;
    wallet branch at :126-137 assigns PaymentProviderCodes.Wallet.
  - Adapter (new): identical decisions, proven by the source-level test
    The_single_provider_authority_is_the_orchestrator_catalog_and_the_adapter_reuses_it, which pins
    "var providerCode = _catalog.DefaultProvider;" and "providerCode = _catalog.DefaultProvider;" in the orchestrator and
    "catalog.DefaultProvider" / "catalog.IsOnlineGatewayOffered()" / "catalog.ManualCardToCardEnabled" in the adapter,
    and forbids any provider/duration literal ("fake"/"gateway"/"wallet"/"manual"/AddHours/TimeSpan.From/Environment.)
    in the adapter.
  - Behavioural proof for `gateway`: test Neutral_gateway_alias_resolves_to_the_exact_catalog_default_provider asserts
    CanonicalProviderCode == catalog.DefaultProvider, RequestedSelector == "gateway", and
    CanonicalProviderCode != "gateway".
  - Order-side behavioural proof: test
    Selected_neutral_gateway_selector_resolves_to_the_concrete_provider_before_hold_preparation asserts
    preparation.LastRequest.ProviderCode == concrete provider (!= "gateway") and
    payments.LastRequest.ProviderCode == "gateway" (trusted original selector preserved).

Why the trusted original selector is preserved at Payment initiation (the task's explicit escape hatch). Passing an
already-concrete code into IPaymentInitiationPort would enter the orchestrator's opaque fall-through branch
(non-manual, non-gateway, non-empty) at :153, and thus SKIP the authoritative `IsOnlineGatewayOffered()` gate and any
`_gateways.Resolve(concreteCode)` would depend on the configured default being a registered gateway. Preserving the
selector keeps Payment as the final eligibility/method authority and keeps 021 idempotency canonicalisation identical
to the direct route. The typed resolution therefore carries BOTH the canonical provider (for 018) and the trusted
original selector (for Payment); no policy is duplicated.

Fail-closed on an absent selector. The composer rejects `UseWallet=false` with a null/blank selector instead of falling
back to the default online gateway, because the mission lists "absent method" as a defended threat and demands
fail-closed on an unavailable/disallowed selector. Unknown non-empty selectors are likewise rejected instead of hitting
the orchestrator's silent DefaultProvider fall-through. The published direct payment route is unchanged. No provider is
ever silently substituted.
