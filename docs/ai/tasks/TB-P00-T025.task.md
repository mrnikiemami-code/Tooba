# A. Core Separation

Hard rules:

```text
Product Review != Seller Review
Review != Rating Aggregate
Review != Catalog
Review != Order
Review != Analytics
```

Catalog owns Product truth.

Seller capability owns Seller identity/lifecycle.

Order owns purchase truth.

Reviews capability owns review/rating submissions and moderation state.

Analytics observes behavior; it does not own ratings.

---

# B. Review Types

At minimum distinguish:

```text
Product Review
Seller Review
```

Potential future types:

```text
Content Review/Feedback
Fulfillment/Delivery Feedback
Support Feedback
```

Do not unify all feedback into one generic table without semantic type boundaries.

Only Product/Seller review architecture is required now.

---

# C. Product Review

A Product Review may reference:


Do not create schema.

Potential factors:

# H. Aggregate Rating

# AU. Decision Summary

Classify:

```text
RECOMMENDED_FOR_ADR
NEEDS_LATER_P00_DETAIL
DEFERRED
```

At minimum recommend:

1. Product Review and Seller Review are distinct concepts.
2. Reviews capability owns submission/moderation truth; Catalog does not own review rows.
3. Rating aggregate is a rebuildable projection, not Product write-model truth.
4. Verified purchase comes through Order/Fulfillment contracts, never cross-module joins.
5. Only published reviews contribute to public/search/SEO/AI projections.
6. Review author identity is separated from public display identity.
7. Single-Store tenant isolation and Marketplace seller/product scopes are explicit.
8. Variant context is preserved without forcing variant-fragmented aggregates.
9. Seller response is separate from review mutation and remains authorization-scoped.
10. Search and SEO consume rating projections only.
11. AI summaries use published sources with provenance and do not fabricate quotes.
12. Moderation actions are SpiceDB-authorized and durably audited.
13. Reviews UI is moderation/workflow/PDP oriented, not CRUD.
14. Rating projections are cacheable but invalidated/versioned from review lifecycle.
15. Abuse controls are explicit extension points, separate from Authorization.
16. Public review data minimizes PII.
17. Review events support Search/SEO/Analytics/Notifications/AI projections.
18. Future deletion/anonymization is possible without collapsing audit/history.
19. Build/test success alone will not constitute UI acceptance.
20. Backend/module boundary does not dictate review UX.

Do not create final ADR yet.
