# B. Pricing Context

A price is always a function of commercial context, not a scalar on Catalog Product or Offer.

Conceptual quote inputs (not all required every time):

```text
ProductId / VariantId
OfferId
SellerId
Market
Sales Channel
Currency
BuyerPartyId
OrganizationId
CommercialAccountId
ContractId
Quantity
Promotion Context
Tax/Fee Context
Requested At / Effective At
Tenant/Store (isolation, not a price)
```

Pricing consumes these via contracts/projections. Never Party/Catalog/Offer table joins. Never Identity locale as a price key.

---

# C. Market

Market is commercial geography/policy: who may be sold to, which books apply, tax-region hints, channel eligibility.

Market is not:

```text
UI language / Locale
ISO money code / Currency
Host / domain / Tenant
Catalog Product identity
```

One Market may support multiple Currencies. One Currency may appear in many Markets. Do not bind locale list in this task.

---

# D. Market Ownership

Market / Commercial Context owns Market definition and market↔channel policies (T002/T004). Status of Market as standalone module vs Pricing-owned context remains:

```text
NEEDS_LATER_P00_DETAIL
```

Direction: do not bury Market inside Catalog, Locale, Tenant, or Identity.

Pricing consumes MarketId. Catalog market eligibility is publication/eligibility, not a price. Offer sellability in a Market is commercial, not Catalog copy.

---

# E. Currency

Currency is an ISO-style money code plus precision/rounding policy, owned as kernel/commercial config, not Locale and not Market.

Display currency may differ from charge currency. That is conversion/checkout policy (section T), not “the product’s currency.”

Unknown or unsupported currency => no quote for charge.

---

# G. Authored Price

An authored price is a human- or system-authored amount in a specific Market + Currency + book/scope (list, seller offer list, contract), with provenance: who authored, when, source book.

Authored prices are Pricing write-model truth for that scope.

Do not silently replace an authored amount with an FX-converted number and call it authored.

---

# H. FX-Derived Price

An FX-derived price is display or conversion using an FX rate from an authored currency to another currency.

Must record:

```text
rate
provider / source id
timestamp / as-of
from currency
to currency
```

Never overwrite authored price with a converted number as if it were authored.

---

# I. FX Provider Boundary

FX is an adapter: internal rate contract, not a vendor SDK inside Pricing domain.

Provider not chosen in this task. Stale or unavailable rates fail closed for charge conversion. Display degradation policy:

```text
NEEDS_LATER_P00_DETAIL
```

Do not treat a third-party FX feed as business truth without snapshot/provenance.

---

# J. Price Book

A Price Book groups authored prices:

```text
book identity
market
currency
sales channel (optional/scoped)
validity window
seller scope and/or contract scope
```

Offer and B2B contracts reference books; they do not copy every cell into Offer or Catalog.

---

# K. Offer Pricing

Align with T008: Catalog does not own authored prices. Offer does not own the quote engine.

Marketplace: each Offer may bind to seller-authored prices in Pricing for that Offer/Seller/Market.

Single-Store: one implicit seller is allowed; do not collapse Price into Catalog Product.

Offer != Price. Changing stock does not change the book. Changing the book does not rewrite Catalog.

---

# L. B2B / Organization Pricing

Preserve T007 seams without implementing B2B:

```text
OrganizationId
CommercialAccountId
ContractId
contract / account price books
quantity breaks
approval-to-buy does not rewrite price books
```

Contract prices are Pricing facts keyed by those opaque ids. Party module does not store list prices. No Party–Pricing table joins.

---

# M. Quantity Pricing

Tiers/breaks are Pricing rules on a book/contract, not Catalog attributes.

Quote must receive Quantity. Do not encode MOQ/tier as a Product field.

---

# N. Promotion Separation

Promotions modify a quote. They are not the base authored price.

Promotion module owns definitions. Pricing applies them as a modifier step in the quote pipeline. Applied promotions are snapshotted on Order.

Price != Promotion. Do not store “sale price” as Catalog truth.

Exact promotion engine:

```text
DEFERRED
```

---

# O. Effective Price / Quote

A Quote is a deterministic result of context + books + promotions + tax/fee policy (if in the quote path) at Effective At.

Public PDP/PLP show a quote or a projection of a quote, never a Product.Price column.

Admin product screens must not treat a single price field as SoT.

---

# P. Quote Validity

Quotes have validity windows and/or version ids.

Cart stores ephemeral quote metadata and must revalidate. Checkout revalidates before capture. A stale quote must not silently become Order truth.

No Cart/Checkout schema in this task.

---

# Q. Order Snapshot

Order persists commercial snapshot needed for history:

```text
amounts and line totals
currency (charge; display if distinct)
market
sales channel
applied price book / contract refs
applied promotions
FX snapshot if conversion used
quote id / version
```

Later book, FX, catalog, or offer mutations must not rewrite historical amounts.

No Order schema in this task.

---

# R. Price History / Audit

Pricing records authored-price changes and quote provenance for audit and ops.

Price history is not a substitute for Order snapshots.

---

# S. Tax / Fee Context

Tax/fee may be quote-time or order-time. Do not bury tax inside Product, Offer, or Locale.

Exact tax engine:

```text
NEEDS_LATER_P00_DETAIL
```

Pricing may accept tax context; it does not become tax SoT.

---

# T. Currency Conversion & Checkout

Charge currency vs display currency: policy later.

Conversion at checkout uses an explicit FX snapshot on the order. Fail closed if a required rate is missing.

Do not implement checkout.

---

# U. Seller Settlement Implications

Seller payout currency/terms are not the customer quote.

Settlement belongs to later marketplace finance. Do not store settlement in Catalog, Offer, or Locale. Do not design payout ledgers in this task.

---

# V. Market / Locale / Domain

Host/tenant resolution (T003) is not Market. Locale is not Market. Domain is not Currency.

Storefront may select Market/Currency under policy after tenant resolve. Do not scatter `if (isMarketplace)` through Pricing.

---

# W. Cache Key Dimensions

Price cache keys must include quote-relevant dimensions, at least:

```text

offer

quantity bucket
promotion-set version

```

Never leak quotes across tenants. Redis is not required initially (T000 caching abstraction). No cache implementation now.

---

# X. Search / PLP / PDP Projection

Search may index display-price projections for a default context. Search is not pricing truth. Sort-by-price is a projection.

PLP consumes denormalized projection fields, not live multi-module SQL.

PDP uses a fresh or short-TTL quote via contract. PDP is not a Pricing table.

This task does not design Search, SEO, or UI.

# AF. Decision Summary

Classify:

```text
RECOMMENDED_FOR_ADR
NEEDS_LATER_P00_DETAIL
DEFERRED
```

At minimum recommend for ADR:

1. Locale != Market != Currency.
2. Product != Price; Offer != Price.
3. Authored price != FX-derived price; FX never silently overwrites authored amounts.
4. Money as decimal or minor-units; never IEEE float.
5. Price books plus a deterministic quote service; Offer/Catalog only reference, they do not own the quote.
6. Quote revalidation before order capture; Cart quote is ephemeral.
7. Order snapshots commercial outcome (amounts, currency, market, books/contracts/promotions, FX if used).
8. Promotions are modifiers, not base authored price.
9. FX behind an internal adapter with provenance; vendor not chosen now.
10. B2B via quote context (Organization / CommercialAccount / Contract), not Party table joins.
11. Search/SEO/PLP consume price projections; they are not Pricing write-model truth.
12. Fail closed on missing or ambiguous price for charge.
13. Market is not Locale, not Tenant/Host, not Currency; do not bury Market in Catalog.
14. Single-Store does not merge price into Catalog Product.
15. Seller settlement currency is not customer quote.

Do not create the final ADR in this task.
