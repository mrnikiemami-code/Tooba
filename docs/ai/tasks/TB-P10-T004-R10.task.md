3. Locked Business Rule — Money Received vs Not Received

Lifecycle decisions must distinguish:

A. Money NOT received

Examples:

online payment never completed

online payment failed and no later success

manual payment order created but customer never submitted payment evidence/reference

These Orders may expire after configured timeout.

B. Money claimed/submitted for manual review

Customer has submitted required manual reference/proof.

This is NOT treated as ordinary unpaid timeout.
Use R5 Manual Review hold/policy.

C. Money confirmed received

Payment Succeeded / Admin confirmed.

Never auto-expire because an old hold timed out.
Use durable supply / EnsureOrderSupply recovery semantics.
