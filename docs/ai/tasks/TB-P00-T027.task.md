## Objective

Design Tooba's Tax architecture based on the USER-approved product policy:

```text
Primary first market: Iran
Near-future market readiness: United Kingdom and additional markets
Base/catalog/commercial prices: tax-exclusive
Tax: calculated separately by Tooba
Tax behavior: market-configurable and extensible
Initial rule model: administrator-defined percentage rules with validity periods
Per-item/context override: allowed only when explicitly enabled by configuration/policy
Tax-exempt treatment: supported
No tax percentage/date/jurisdiction law hard-coded in domain/application logic
B2B tax/VAT/invoice-specific functionality: NOT in initial phase
```

Important:

The example provided by USER:

```text
10% from 1/1/1405 to 10/10/1406
```

is a product example of a configurable date-effective rule.

It is NOT a statement of current Iranian tax law and MUST NOT be encoded as a legal default.

This task is architecture documentation only.

Do not implement tax calculation code, tax tables, admin UI, schemas, migrations, jurisdiction providers, B2B VAT, invoices, or template integration.

# B. Core Separation

Hard rules:

```text
Tax != Pricing
Tax != Promotion
Tax != Order
Tax != Payment
Tax != Invoice
Tax != Jurisdiction Law Source
```

Pricing owns tax-exclusive commercial/base pricing.

Tax owns tax determination/calculation policy and tax calculation result.

Promotion owns incentives/adjustments.

Order snapshots accepted tax calculation results.

Payment executes financial collection/refund.

Future Invoice capability may consume tax evidence but does not become Tax authority.

# AZ. Decision Summary

Classify:

```text
RECOMMENDED_FOR_ADR
NEEDS_LATER_P00_DETAIL
DEFERRED
```

At minimum recommend:

1. Tax is separate from Pricing, Promotion, Order and Payment.
2. Initial commercial prices are tax-exclusive.
3. Tooba calculates Tax separately in first release.
4. Tax rules are configurable data, never hard-coded jurisdiction/rate logic.
5. Rules are effective-dated and versioned.
6. Iran is first-market emphasis, but core architecture is jurisdiction-neutral and UK/multi-market ready.
7. Locale, Market, Currency and Tax Jurisdiction remain distinct.
8. Catalog references Tax Category but never calculates Tax.
9. Tax Exempt, Zero Rate, Missing Rule and Calculation Error are distinct outcomes.
10. Manual/context rate override is disabled unless policy explicitly enables it.
11. Manual overrides require SpiceDB authorization, reason and durable audit.
12. Tax calculation is deterministic and explainable.
13. Order snapshots applied tax evidence; historical orders never use current rates.
14. Payment consumes final total and does not calculate Tax.
15. Returns uses historical Tax snapshot for refund coordination.
16. Missing applicable Tax Rule does not silently become zero.
17. Rule conflicts are deterministic and never depend on row order.
18. Initial one-percentage rule UX does not prevent multiple Tax Components later.
19. Single-Store tenant-specific Tax configuration works within one shared publish.
20. B2B Tax/VAT/invoice functionality is excluded from initial phase.
21. Tax Admin UX is a Tax Configuration Workspace, not raw CRUD.
22. Tax UI/price presentation is market-policy-aware and cannot mislead final payable price.
23. Backend/module boundary != UI boundary.
24. Future Tax UI requires visual evidence and Architect visual acceptance.

Do not create final ADR yet.
