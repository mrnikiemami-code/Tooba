# B. Catalog Product != Seller Offer

P00 must keep two identities:

```text
Catalog Product = canonical sellable identity and descriptive/merchandisable truth
Seller Offer / Listing = seller-specific commercial presentation bound to catalog identity
```

Do not prematurely merge them because Single-Store has one seller, because a template SKU table mixed them, or because PDP shows both.

An Offer references Catalog Product / Variant by opaque id. An Offer does not become the Catalog Product.

---

# C. Catalog Product Model Direction

Catalog owns conceptually:

```text
canonical product identity
descriptive attributes and copy references
variant structure
brand / category classification references
publication / lifecycle status of catalog identity
media/content references by contract (not the bytes or CMS body)
```

Catalog does not own:

```text
seller commercial lifecycle
authored market prices
inventory quantities or reservations
search index internals
SEO URL ownership as the only truth
page layout trees
```

Inheritance vs table strategy is not locked.

---

# D. Product vs Variant

Product is the sellable identity family. Variant is a distinguishable option set under that family (size, color, pack, and similar).

A Variant is not automatically:

```text
a price
a stock row
an Offer
a search document
```

Cardinality (one product many variants; variant-less products; option axes) is conceptual. Exact option-axis model:

```text
NEEDS_LATER_P00_DETAIL
```

---

# E. Attributes

Distinguish:

```text
Product-level attributes (shared across variants)
Variant-level attributes (discriminators)
Facet/filter attributes for discovery projections
Offer-level commercial flags (not catalog descriptive truth)
```

Attribute dictionaries are Catalog-owned where they describe product identity. Offer must not fork canonical attribute truth. Faceted search consumes projections, not live Catalog tables.

Do not lock an EAV vs typed-column schema.

---

# F. Brand / Manufacturer

Brand is a catalog classification/identity helper, not a Seller and not a Party.

A manufacturer Party may later exist; Brand display is not automatically that Party. Exact Brand ownership vs Party:

```text
NEEDS_LATER_P00_DETAIL
```

Do not merge Brand into Seller profile.

---

# G. Category / Taxonomy

Category/taxonomy supports navigation, merchandising assignment, and later facets.

Catalog owns product classification facts. Merchandising may assign presentation collections without rewriting product identity.

Multiple trees, locale-specific labels, and market-specific visible categories may exist later without binding Locale to Market.

No schema.

---

# H. Seller Model

Seller is a marketplace commercial participant.

Align with T007:

```text
Seller / Marketplace owns seller commercial lifecycle
Party owns business identity backing the seller
SpiceDB owns seller-scoped authorization decisions
Tenant/Store is deployment/platform context, not the seller
```

Seller does not own Catalog Product identity. Seller operators may create/manage Offers and, under policy, propose catalog contributions — not silently rewrite global product truth.

---

# I. Offer / Listing Model

Offer owns conceptually:

```text
listing identity
seller binding
catalog product/variant binding
listing-level commercial flags / status
optional seller-specific presentation overlays that are not canonical catalog copy
```

Offer does not own:

```text
canonical product description as source of truth
global catalog identity
price books
inventory write model
search engine internals
```

Pricing and Inventory reference Offer (and/or SKU) by opaque id via contracts.

---

# J. Offer–Product Binding

Binding is explicit. One Catalog Product/Variant may have many Offers (marketplace). One Offer binds to one canonical catalog identity (product or variant — exact cardinality `NEEDS_LATER_P00_DETAIL`).

Unbound listings, pending catalog match, and contribution/approval seams must be preserved as future flows. No matching engine now.

Forbidden: Offer id used as Catalog Product id.

---

# K. SKU / Sellable Unit

Analyze SKU as a commercial/fulfillment identifier vs Catalog Variant vs Offer.

Candidates (not locked): SKU owned by Catalog Variant; SKU owned by Offer; seller SKU distinct from platform SKU.

Order/Cart/Inventory must not depend on a merged Product=SKU=Offer row.

Exact SKU owner:

```text
NEEDS_LATER_P00_DETAIL
```

---

# L. Single-Store vs Marketplace

Marketplace: Seller module REQUIRED; many sellers; many Offers per catalog identity.

Single-Store: Seller module DISABLED/overlay; one implicit seller is allowed.

Hard rule:

```text
Single-Store must not merge Catalog Product and Offer data models
```

Composition/policy hides marketplace UX. Do not scatter `if (isMarketplace)` through Catalog. Do not drop Offer because there is one store.

---

# M. Pricing Separation

Never model product price as one scalar on Catalog Product.

Pricing consumes Offer/Catalog ids plus Market, Sales Channel, Currency, Party/Organization, Quantity, Contract, Promotion, validity — via contracts, never Catalog–Pricing table joins.

Catalog may carry non-authoritative display hints only if clearly not SoT. Authored price lives in Pricing (later P00 package).

This task does not design Pricing.

---

# N. Inventory Separation

Availability is not catalog copy.

Inventory owns stock/reservation write model keyed by Offer and/or SKU refs via contract. Catalog does not store sellable quantity as truth.

This task does not design Inventory.

---

# O. Content, Media, Search Separation

```text
Content = semantic/editorial write model (not Blog-only)
Media = assets and derivatives
Search = index/query over feeds/projections
Catalog = product identity
```

Catalog stores opaque Media/Content refs. Search/AI consume feeds. PostgreSQL FTS now / Elasticsearch later must not leak into Catalog domain logic.

This task does not design Content, Media, Search, or SEO.

---

# P. PDP Composition

Product Detail Page is a storefront composition: Catalog projection + Offer(s) + Pricing quote + Inventory availability + Media + Reviews + Content slots + merchandising.

PDP is not a Catalog table and not a reason to join Catalog, Offer, Pricing, and Inventory.

Admin product screens follow the same ownership rule.

---

# Q. PLP Composition

Product Listing Page is a discovery composition: Search/merchandising projection + facet attributes + Offer/availability/price display fields as denormalized projection — not live multi-module SQL.

PLP ranking is merchandising/search, not Catalog identity.

---

# R. Merchandising vs Catalog

Merchandising owns presentation: collections, featured ranking, badges as presentation, campaign placement.

It does not own canonical product identity, prices, or stock.

Badges that are commercial (e.g. “official seller”) may be Offer/Seller facts projected to merchandising — ownership stays with the source module.

---

# S. Locale / Translation

Product is multilingual. Locale != Market != Currency.

Catalog copy/attributes have locale variants. Market availability and authored prices are not translations.

Do not bind locale list in this task. Initial locale list remains unresolved.

---

# T. Market / Channel Availability

A Catalog Product may be globally defined while Offers, Pricing, and Inventory determine whether it is sellable in a Market / Sales Channel / Tenant.

Catalog may hold eligibility flags only as Catalog-owned publication facts, not as price or stock.

Exact market-eligibility owner:

```text
NEEDS_LATER_P00_DETAIL
```

Do not bury Market inside Catalog.

---

# U. B2B Readiness

Preserve seams without implementing B2B:

```text
organization-specific assortments / catalogs
contract-priced offers (Pricing, not Catalog)
approval-to-buy does not rewrite product identity
seller that is also a B2B buyer (Party roles)
private/restricted listings
```

Catalog remains identity. Contract/price book remains Pricing/commercial. Party/Organization remains T007.

---

# V. Authorization Integration

SpiceDB may gate:

```text
catalog product management scope
seller offer management
catalog contribution / bind listing
admin merchandising
```

Catalog Product management scope != Catalog Product identity. Seller relations do not grant canonical product-write.

Business lifecycle of Product/Offer remains in Catalog/Offer modules. SpiceDB is not a product dump.

---

# X. Identifier / Reference Policy

Opaque ids across Catalog, Offer, Seller, Media, Content.

No FK-driven ownership across modules. No leaking internal surrogates in public contracts as a requirement.

OfferId != ProductId != VariantId != SellerId != PartyId != TenantId.

---

# Y. Lifecycle / Status

Preserve independent lifecycles:

```text
Catalog Product: draft / published / archived (candidate names, not locked)
Variant: active / discontinued
Offer: listed / paused / delisted
Seller: onboarding / active / suspended
```

Delisting an Offer must not delete Catalog Product. Archiving a Product must not silently destroy historical Order snapshots.

No state-machine implementation.

---

# Z. Events / Contracts Outbound

Catalog/Offer emit facts for Search, AI, Pricing context, Inventory keys, Analytics ProductView — via contracts/events/feeds.

Not every internal change is an integration event.

No broker, outbox, or schema lock in this task.

---

# AA. Duplicate / Merge / Canonicalization

Preserve future: duplicate product detection, merge, offer rebind, GTIN/MPN matching, seller contribution to existing canonical product.

Primary keys are not the only identity semantics. External identifiers (GTIN, MPN, seller SKU) are references, not module-crossing FKs.

---

# AB. Roles in Catalog Commerce

Cover at least:

```text
Catalog admin / merchandiser
Marketplace seller operator
Single-store implicit operator
B2C shopper on PDP/PLP
B2B buyer seeing restricted assortment
```

Actor vs Buyer remains T007. This section does not redesign Party.

---

# AC. Cart / Order Snapshot of Catalog & Offer

Cart stores Offer/Product/Variant refs plus ephemeral display/price metadata; revalidate.

Order snapshots commercial and display facts needed for history (title, variant label, seller, offer id, identifiers). Historical truth must not depend on later Catalog/Offer mutation.

No Cart/Order schema here.

Analyze Marketplace vs Single-Store vs Future B2B for:

```text
Seller module
Offer / Listing
Implicit single seller
Restricted/private assortment
Catalog contribution

```

Shared Catalog module. Edition differences are composition/policy.

Purchased template and Shopeiva demo catalog/SKU/vendor tables are adaptation input, not architecture truth.

Do not promote template feature mix (product=price=stock=vendor on one row) into Tooba requirements.

Digikala/Amazon are references, not a model to copy.

At minimum recommend for ADR:

1. Catalog Product separate from Seller Offer / Listing.
2. Product separate from Variant; Variant not a price or stock row.
3. Seller separate from Party and from Tenant/Store.
4. Single-Store implicit seller without merging Catalog and Offer models.
5. Catalog does not own authored prices.
6. Catalog does not own inventory quantities.
7. Search/AI consume projections/feeds, not Catalog tables as truth.
8. Content and Media referenced by opaque id; not owned as Catalog blobs/CMS.
9. PDP and PLP are compositions/projections, not Catalog persistence.
10. Merchandising does not own product identity.
11. Locale != Market != Currency for product copy vs commercial context.
12. Offer binds to Catalog identity by opaque id; OfferId is not ProductId.
13. SpiceDB catalog-management scope is not Catalog Product identity.
14. Order snapshots catalog/offer display facts; live catalog is not historical truth.
15. B2B assortment/contract seams preserved without implementing B2B now.

Do not create the final ADR in this task.
