# A. Core Separation

Hard rules:

```text
AI != Business Source of Truth
AI != Search Source of Truth
AI != Authorization System
AI != Direct Database Client
```

AI consumes approved sources and contracts.

Business modules remain authoritative.

SpiceDB remains authorization decision system.

---

# B. AI Use Cases

Preserve future use cases such as:

```text
customer product discovery
product comparison
shopping guidance
FAQ/help
content Q&A
order/help guidance where authorized
seller assistant future
admin assistant future
content drafting assistance future
```

Do not promote every possible AI feature into launch scope.

Classify customer-facing assistant as current strategic requirement; seller/admin copilots may be future.

---

# C. Grounding Principle

Hard requirement:

AI answers about Tooba products/content/business facts must be grounded in approved sources.

Conceptual:

```text
User Question
→ Context Resolution
→ Authorization
→ Retrieval
→ Grounded Context
→ Model
→ Response + Provenance
```

Do not allow model memory alone to assert mutable Tooba facts such as:

```text
price
stock
seller availability
order status
policy
published content
```

---

# D. Knowledge Sources

Define approved source classes.

At minimum:

```text
Published Content
Catalog projections
Product specifications
Brand/category content
FAQ/help
Public seller facts where approved
Pricing quote/read contract for live price where appropriate
Inventory/availability read contract where appropriate
Order/customer facts only when authorized
```

Do not directly expose raw module tables.

---

# E. Published Content Source

Content is a primary RAG source.

Only use:

```text
approved
published
eligible for AI retrieval
correct tenant/store
correct locale/audience
```

Draft/internal/unpublished content must not leak.

Preserve revision/version metadata.

---

# F. Catalog Source

AI may retrieve normalized Catalog projections such as:

```text
ProductId
localized title
specifications
brand
category
variant facts
approved descriptive data
```

Catalog remains authoritative.

AI/vector store is projection only.

# G. Pricing / Inventory Freshness

Dynamic facts require caution.

Do not embed stale static price/stock and treat them as durable truth.

For live questions such as:

```text
How much is this?
Is it in stock?
```

architecture should prefer live/internal read contracts or freshness-aware projections.

Need clear distinction:

```text
semantic retrieval
```

vs:

```text
live business fact lookup
```

# H. Search vs AI Retrieval

Hard separation:

```text
Product Search Index != automatically AI Knowledge Index
```

General Search optimizes:

```text
query/result ranking/facets
```

AI retrieval may optimize:

```text

semantic relevance
grounding
provenance
authorization
```

They may share source projections but not be architecturally conflated.

# O. Authorization-Aware Retrieval

This is mandatory.

Before private/customer/seller/admin data reaches the model:

```text
authenticate principal
resolve tenant/context
authorize resource/scope
retrieve only permitted data
```

Do not retrieve everything and ask the LLM to "hide forbidden data."

Authorization happens before/at retrieval boundary.

Separate:

```text

admin context
```

Each has different retrieval/tool permissions.

Do not reuse a broad admin index for public chat.

Marketplace:

Use marketplace deployment/resource/seller/customer scopes appropriately.

Hard rule:

No cross-tenant retrieval due to missing filter.


# BD. Decision Summary

Classify:

```text
RECOMMENDED_FOR_ADR
NEEDS_LATER_P00_DETAIL
DEFERRED
```

At minimum recommend:

1. AI is not business/search/authorization truth.
2. Customer AI answers are grounded on approved sources.
3. Content AI retrieval uses only published/approved/eligible revisions.
4. Catalog facts enter via projections/contracts.
5. Dynamic price/inventory use freshness-aware live reads where needed.
6. General Search index and AI knowledge index are separate concerns.
7. AI retrieval/provider infrastructure is hidden behind internal abstractions.
8. Authorization happens before/at retrieval, never delegated to the LLM.
9. Tenant isolation applies to chunks/indexes/cache/conversations/tools.
10. Locale/Market/Currency remain separate AI context.
11. Provenance/citations are preserved.
12. Retrieved/user content is untrusted data, not system instruction.
13. AI has no direct DB mutation/access path.
14. State-changing actions use authorized application commands/tools.
15. Sensitive/high-impact actions support explicit confirmation policy.
16. Prompt/model/embedding/retriever/tool schemas are versioned.
17. AI indexes are rebuildable from authoritative sources.
18. AI provider failure does not break core commerce.
19. Analytics/recommendation signals enter through controlled interfaces.
20. AI UI/UX is commerce-aware, cited, mobile/RTL/a11y capable.
21. AI quality requires automated evaluation including authorization-leak tests.
22. Mass AI-generated SEO content cannot bypass Content/SEO governance.

Do not create final ADR yet.
